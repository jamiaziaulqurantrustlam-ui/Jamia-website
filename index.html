ABOUT THE INSTITUTION

Jamia Zia Ul Quran Trust LAM

Jamia is committed to providing Quranic, Islamic and general education in a disciplined and nurturing environment, serving students from local and surrounding communities.

  • Quranic and Islamic education
  • General education support
  • Hostel and day-scholar facilities
  • Future expansion towards Alim course
