JAMIA ZIA UL QURAN TRUST LAM

Jamia Zia Ul Quran Trust LAM is committed to providing Quranic, Islamic and general education in a disciplined and nurturing environment. The institution serves students from local and surrounding communities with residential and day-scholar facilities.

  • Quranic and Islamic education
  • General education support
  • Hostel and day-scholar facilities
  • Future expansion towards Alim course

Principal's Message
