OUR PURPOSE

THE STUDENTS WE AIM TO NURTURE

Our Educational Purpose

Jamia Zia Ul Quran aims to prepare students who are deeply connected to the Holy Quran, practise Islamic teachings, excel in ethics and character, understand foundational contemporary subjects, and use their academic and literary abilities positively.

  • A deep connection with the Holy Quran
  • Living by Islamic teachings
  • Excellent ethics and character
  • Awareness of foundational contemporary subjects and positive use of academic and literary abilities
